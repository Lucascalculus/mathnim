## Design
- World (passes INTERACTION-BAR in plan): causes = Choice "what the QP is built around"
  (straight ahead = cold / last plan shifted = warm), Choice "QPs per sample" (1, 2, 3, converge),
  slider "instant to inspect". The map shows the reference dots, the guess (dotted), the QP
  prediction (dashed purple), the plan's true motion (thin ink), and the driven path. On the right:
  e(t) with a ±15 cm brief band, plus readouts (max|e|, brief met, mean QPs, slowest speed).
  Entry state is cold/1 (brief not met).
  - Goal 1 'rti': within 15 cm at exactly 1 QP/sample (only warm/1).
  - Goal 2 'cold-cheapest': cold start within the brief with the fewest QPs (cold/2).
  - Detectors: 'nmpc-must-converge' (picks conv: same path, more QPs) and 'linearise-anywhere'
    (back to cold/1: the prediction peels away from the truth).
  - Prediction checkpoint before the world: one cold QP vs one warm QP in the bend.
- Planned visual 2: `mpc_shooting` scene, two panels (single vs multiple shooting iterates 0..3
  from `shooting_iterates`), with the gaps drawn in coral.
- Planned figure: RTI preparation/feedback timeline SVG en/zh in apps/mathnim/public/mpc/m8/.
- Lesson steps (file apps/mathnim/src/lessons/mpc/m8_nmpc.ts, id mpc-m8-nmpc):
  1. read why-nonlinear: bicycle equations, what each term does, what the model leaves out
     (slip; valid below 0.5µg lateral acceleration, Polack 2017; Kong 2015); the S peaks at
     4 m/s² ≈ 0.41 g;
  2. read/derive nmpc problem: no longer a QP, local solutions (Ipopt); linear MPC = RTI with
     preparation done once offline (Gros 2020);
  3. scene shooting, plus read single vs multiple (RMD §8.5.2);
  4. derive SQP: X ≈ X̄ + GΔU (G plays Γ's role from M2), Gauss–Newton H, solve, step, repeat;
  5. read RTI: one QP per sample, shifted warm start, preparation/feedback (Diehl 2002, Gros 2020);
  6. checkpoint predict, then the world explore, then the takeaway;
  7. code: CasADi Opti multiple-shooting NMPC (casadi not installed; untested);
  8. read tools (CasADi, Ipopt, acados, do-mpc) and applications (Kamel: 0.45 vs 2.35 ms, RMSE
     7.1 vs 10.8 cm, not a general rule; Falcone 2007; Qin & Badgwell 93 vs 4542);
  9. read learning + MPC (Hewing's 3 categories, Rosolia LMPC, NN approximation, Recht);
     mark as contested;
  10. read 怎么选: a table PID/LQR/LMPC/NMPC/RL × model/compute/constraints/data
      (research-c.md "Facts for the decision page"), typical domains, a decision path,
      counterexamples;
  11. 3–4 checks: number (0.5µg validity), choice (warm guess), choice (pick a controller),
      text (why RTI is enough).
- Sources: all in docs/lessons/mpc/research-c.md §4–8 and the decision-page section; write
  research-m8.md as an index into it.
