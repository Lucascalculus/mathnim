## Lesson design (file `m2_model.ts`, id `mpc-m2-model`, mode essay)
1. read, "Why MPC needs a model": M1's planner predicted the future, and prediction needs a model.
2. read/derive, "From physics to discrete time":
   - double integrator ṗ = v, v̇ = a;
   - ZOH: the input is held over each step;
   - exact discretization gives A and B;
   - embed visual `mpc_zoh_step` (rectangle Δt·v_k plus triangle ½Δt²a_k; Euler keeps only the rectangle);
   - what A means (coasting: position gains Δt·v) and what B means (one held push: Δt in speed, ½Δt² in position).
3. derive, "Stack the predictions":
   - x1, x2, x3, then the general x_k = A^k x0 + Σ_{j<k} A^{k−1−j} B u_j;
   - X = Φx0 + ΓU, with Φ = (A, A², …) and Γ block (k,j) = A^{k−1−j}B for j < k;
   - Γ is lower triangular because of causality: u_j cannot affect earlier states.
   - The cart's blocks: [(k−j−½)Δt², Δt].
   - Blank to fill: a block of Γ.
4. checkpoint (predict): same total braking, early vs late. Is the final speed the same, and which goes farther? Correct answer: same speed, late goes farther. Misconception id `timing-irrelevant`.
5. scene, reveal: `mpc_bars` with `{ story: 'early_late' }`, narrated.
6. explore, world `mpc_bars` (plan 'zero'):
   - Goals: `at_rest` (s.status().at_rest) and `docked` (s.status().docked).
   - Events: 'bars' from `scene.listen` via common.ts `attach('mpc_bars')`.
   - Detectors:
     - (a) at_rest && past_k > 0 → misconception `timing-irrelevant`. Line: 速度只看总量，位置还看什么时候踩 / "speed only counts the total; position also cares when you brake".
     - (b) at_rest && short_by > 0.01 → finding "braked too early". Line: move braking later.
     - (c) v_end < −0.006 → misconception `more-brake-safer`. Line: more than −5 in total reverses the cart.
   - Cue: `[bar_label(0)]` (the labels come from `bar_label` in bars.ts, en and zh).
   - solve: `s.set_plan(DOCK_PLAN)` and emit 'bars' with `s.report(0)`.
   - Takeaway read: the stopping identity t_c = 0.6 s; the last rows of Γ.
7. read, "The key insight": X is linear (affine) in U, so a quadratic cost stays quadratic in U (M3), and linear limits on X and U stay linear, so the problem is a QP (M4). Also why not simulate nonlinearly: here it is exact and cheap.
8. Checks (3–4):
   - number: the Euler-planned plan's real end, 9.975 m;
   - number or choice: v10 for a given plan;
   - choice: why Γ is lower triangular (causality);
   - text: why linearity in U matters (QP).

## Sources (verified 2026-10-26)
- MathWorks, "Continuous-Discrete Conversion Methods": https://www.mathworks.com/help/control/ug/continuous-discrete-conversion-methods.html. ZOH "provides an exact match ... in the time domain" for staircase inputs, holding each u(k) over one period.
- Bemporad, "Model Predictive Control" course slides, linear MPC part: https://cse.lab.imtlucca.it/~bemporad/teaching/mpc/imt/1-linear_mpc.pdf (slides 36–37). They give x_k = A^k x0 + Σ A^j B u_{k−1−j} and the stacked X = S̄z + T̄x0, with S̄ block lower triangular and the condensed H, F.
- Rawlings, Mayne and Diehl, *Model Predictive Control: Theory, Computation, and Design*, 2nd ed.: https://sites.engineering.ucsb.edu/~jbraw/mpc/ (free PDFs; verified).
