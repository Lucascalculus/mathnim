## Lesson 1, value (Week 1)
- Value is perceived, relative, dynamic (caviar and handbag examples, qualitatively): puzzle, name.
- Drucker (customer rarely buys what the firm thinks it sells; purpose is to create and keep customers; marketing and innovation) and Keynes: name.
- Value creation summary, cycle of firm, customer and stakeholder value, trade-offs: name, neighbour.
- Zeithaml's four meanings; fast fashion as low price: name.
- Means-end model and Rokeach terminal and instrumental values: touch2 (ladder).
- B2C versus B2B (Smith and Colgate; Ulaga and Eggert); costs as time, effort and psychic cost; price as weakest differentiation; ratio versus summative value; B2B result types: name.
- Four types of value, and "pen users: it depends on who the customer is": name.
- Sources of value (product, information, environment, interactions, ownership): touch3.
- Costs in the value equation (price, risk, effort, time): name, recall.

## Lesson 2, market orientation (Week 4, first half)
- Market orientation (Kirca et al.: information acquisition and dissemination, interfunctional judgement, shared diagnosis, coordinated action, customer, competitor and other market information; links to innovativeness, loyalty, quality, performance): name.
- Responsive versus proactive (Narver et al.): touch, name.
- Post-it story: puzzle. Customer-led is not market-oriented (orientation, style, focus, goal, learning behaviour, learning process): touch, name.
- Market-driven versus driving markets (Jaworski et al.): deconstruction, construction, functional modification: touch2.
- Market-driven organisation (Day): sensing, linking, bonding; agility, explore and exploit; adaptive versus transformative change; change programme: name.

## Lesson 3, service logic (Week 4, second half)
- S-D logic (Vargo and Lusch), customer as co-creator, goods as appliances: name.

- Gronroos "companies do not deliver value, customers do", value-in-use, where value creation takes place: name.
- Unit of analysis (output versus relationship), orientation (producer versus consumer): touch, name.
